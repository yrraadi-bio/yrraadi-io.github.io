· · i Pathway score: mean training-standardized log1p-CPM expression of the pathway's genes measured on a tile, positive above the training average and negative below. Feature norm: L2 norm of the 16-dimensional coordinate a feature assigns to a patch, zero wherever the feature is not among that patch's active features; a tile's figure is the log1p of that norm averaged over its 196 patches. Raw r: Pearson correlation between that score and the selected feature's tile norm across training tiles. Held-out refers to the 12 unseen slides and their tiles.
